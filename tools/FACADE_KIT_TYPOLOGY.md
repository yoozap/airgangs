Viewed 47 images across all subdirs and the finished tile set. The corpus splits roughly: Soviet microdistrict panel/block ~35%, old-Tbilisi historic ~20%, classic Vake-Vera plaster (Stalinist/19c eclectic) ~20%, modern new-builds ~15%, shopfront/industrial/other ~10%. About a third of the thumbs are non-facade scenes (buses, plazas, kiosks) — the `public/textures/pb` tiles are the reliable reference.

# TBILISI FACADE DETAIL KIT — TYPOLOGY CATALOGUE

## A. WINDOW ARCHETYPES (6)

**W1. Old-Tbilisi tall wooden French casement (often arched)**
- Districts: old town (Kala/Sololaki/Avlabari), restored courtyards. Frequency: ~15% of windows in corpus.
- Proportions: 0.95–1.1 w × 2.2–2.6 h m (door-window opening onto balcony); segmental-arch top on restored examples.
- Frame: honey pine `#B07A3C` (restored) or dark walnut `#5C3A22` (aged); wall behind yellow plaster `#ECD9A8`.
- Mullions: 2 leaves × 3 vertical panes each + 1–2 pane arched transom; thin 40mm glazing bars. Lace curtains behind glass are a signature.
- Details: no external sill to speak of; interior fretwork valance visible at top pane.
- Exemplars: `public/textures/pb/b27226.jpg`, `b20206.jpg` (upper floor), `b172431.jpg` (behind gallery).

**W2. Stalinist / neoclassic tall casement with molded surround**
- Districts: Vake-Vera, Rustaveli/Marjanishvili classic plaster blocks. Frequency: ~12%.
- Proportions: 1.1–1.3 × 2.1–2.3 m, floor height 3.2–3.6 m.
- Frame: brown wood `#5F4630` or aged grey-brown `#6E5B45`; plaster field cream `#D9CDB4` or grey `#B7B0A4`, surrounds white `#E8E4DA`.
- Mullions: 2 leaves × 2–3 panes + transom; surround = flat pilaster strips + triangular or segmental pediment hood; projecting sill on consoles (150mm).
- Exemplars: `b47132.jpg` (pediments, cornice, balustrade), `b185430.jpg`, `b13463.jpg` (round oculus variant, Corinthian pilasters).

**W3. Soviet brown-wood double casement (panel/khrushchevka stock)**
- Districts: Gldani, Varketili, Saburtalo microdistricts, Nutsubidze. Frequency: highest of the "original" types, ~20%.
- Proportions: 1.45 × 1.5 m (2-leaf) and 1.7–2.1 × 1.5 m (3-part with wide center); floor height 2.8 m; recessed 100–150mm into panel with visible seam frame around opening.
- Frame: brown wood `#6B4A2F`–`#7A5230`; panel wall `#CFC4AD` with light seam lines `#E0DACB`.
- Mullions: 2 casements, each single tall pane (occasionally 1 horizontal bar); 3-part adds fixed center.
- Details: flush concrete sill, dark weather streaks under; mix-and-match with W4 on the same facade is essential for realism.
- Exemplars: `b11614.jpg` (perfect reference sheet), `b34.jpg`, thumbs `r4a2/117650.jpg`, `r4a4/124.jpg`.

**W4. White PVC replacement (2-pane)**
- Districts: everywhere — ~40% of all windows in corpus, on every building age.
- Proportions: fills W2/W3 openings; typical 1.4 × 1.4 m; 60mm frame + 80mm center mullion.
- Frame: bright white `#F2F2EE`, dark open-gap `#20242A`; often half-open tilt.
- Mullions: 1 fixed + 1 casement; larger = 3-part. School/public variant: big 4×3 grid (`b3470.jpg`, pink plaster `#F0D7D2`).
- Exemplars: `b89386.jpg`, `b3470.jpg`, `b51207.jpg` (ribbon of sliders on institutional beige).

**W5. Brick-eclectic sash with decorative brick hood + louvered shutters**
- Districts: 19c red-brick industrial/eclectic (Marjanishvili, Chugureti, rail district). Frequency: ~8%.
- Proportions: 1.0 × 1.8 m; brick field `#A8563A`–`#B0603F`, mortar `#C9B9A4`.
- Mullions: 1-over-1 sash or 2-leaf; steel-frame industrial variant 4×6 small panes (`b14552.jpg`, frame black-grey `#3B3B3B`, teal reveals `#2E7F72`).
- Details: corbelled brick "crown" hood over lintel, dentil brick cornice bands, projecting sill course; olive louvered wooden shutters `#9A9455` swung open.
- Exemplars: `b107619.jpg` (hoods + shutters), `b14552.jpg`, `b129960.jpg` (modern re-use with glass-block slits).

**W6. Modern aluminum tinted / shopfront glazing**
- Districts: new infill and ground floors citywide. Frequency: ~12%.
- Proportions: window-walls 2.4–3.6 w × 2.2 h m; ground shopfront full-height 3.0–3.5 m.
- Frame: black/bronze aluminum `#33383B`; glass green-blue tint `#7FA8A0` or deep blue mirrored `#2B3F8C` (`b3944.jpg`).
- Details: shop fascia band 0.8m high (red `#C1272D` Aversi, `b81417.jpg`), louvered vent panels, diamond-mesh security grilles on ground floor.
- Exemplars: `b66371.jpg`, `b8444.jpg`, `b3944.jpg`, `b81417.jpg`.

## B. BALCONY ARCHETYPES (4 + juliet)

**B1. Carved wooden gallery ("shushabandi" family) — old town signature**
- Full-width deck 1.2–1.5 m deep on carved wood corbels; square posts ~100×100mm to roof; balustrade 0.9 m of fretwork-cut panels (floral scroll or slot-and-lozenge pattern) between rails; dentil/scallop trim board under deck edge; beadboard ceiling.
- Colors: white `#EDEAE2` (`b27226.jpg`), sage green `#6F9E8A` on brick `#B96B4E` (`b172431.jpg` — the definitive modeling reference), raw grey wood on decayed ones.
- Districts: Kala, Sololaki, Avlabari, Betlemi rise. ~15% of corpus buildings.
- Exemplars: `b172431.jpg`, `b27226.jpg`, thumb `r4a5/98301.jpg` (top-right).

**B2. Soviet welded metal-bar balcony / open loggia rail**
- 12mm bars at ~110mm spacing, flat-bar top rail, 0.95 m high; infill variants: none, flat sheet, corrugated sheet (galvanized `#9AA0A3` or rusted `#8A5B3C`), wired-on mesh. Cantilever slab 0.9–1.05 m deep with drip-stained edge.
- Colors: white paint `#E5E3DC` chipping to rust; slab concrete `#B5AEA0`.
- Districts: all Soviet housing. The single most common balcony — ~40%.
- Exemplars: `b34.jpg`, `b190295.jpg` (continuous 60s galleries + green windbreak mesh `#3F6B3A`), `b124.jpg`, thumb `r4a4/124.jpg`.

**B3. Precast concrete loggia panel (often repainted)**
- Recessed loggia 2.4–3.2 w × 1.0 h m panel; either flat with ribs, or cast lattice/brick-lattice pattern.
- Colors seen: magenta-purple lattice `#7E3A5A` (`b119011.jpg`), red-orange `#C25B45` alternating white (thumb `r4a1/1251.jpg`), sky blue `#4F97AD` (`b2246.jpg` — whole 16-story facade), unpainted `#B5AEA0`.
- Districts: Gldani/Varketili high panels, Vazha-Pshavela quarters. ~25%.
- Exemplars: `b2246.jpg`, `b119011.jpg`, thumb `r4a1/1251.jpg`.

**B4. Glazed-in veranda add-on (self-built)**
- Existing balcony wrapped in glazing: wood small-pane grid (old, `#8B6A45`, 6–12 panes per bay with lace curtains — `b96458.jpg`) or white alu/PVC (`b124.jpg` bottom); often sits on self-built red-brick or block extension bulging past the facade line (thumb `r4a2/117650.jpg`).
- Districts: every Soviet courtyard; ~30% of balconies are partially glazed. Adds great silhouette chaos.
- Exemplars: `b96458.jpg`, `b124.jpg`, thumbs `r4a2/117650.jpg`, `r4a4/124.jpg`.

**B5 (minor). Wrought-iron juliet / bow balcony** — Vake-Vera classic: cast-iron lace panels `#2E2E30` (`b47132.jpg`, `b20206.jpg`) or "fish-belly" bowed vertical bars (`b185430.jpg`, `b93657.jpg`); 0.4 m projection, stone balustrade variant with turned balusters on carved corbels also in `b47132.jpg`.

## C. OTHER FACADE DETAILS WORTH MODELING (6)

1. **Drainpipes** — on nearly every facade: galvanized steel `#9AA0A3` with conical joints and kinked elbows (`b185430.jpg`), white PVC `#E8E8E4` (`b5091.jpg`, thumb `r4a0/103196.jpg`), decorative red-painted `#A03A30` on restored old town (`b27226.jpg`). 100mm dia, standoff brackets every ~1.5 m.
2. **Panel-block entrance** — recessed dark doorway + cantilevered concrete slab canopy (1.2×2.5 m, mossy edge), blue/white address plate `#2D6FB8`, painted-over graffiti patch, step + asphalt apron: thumb `r4a0/103196.jpg` is a complete kit reference; maroon granite portal for public buildings (`b336.jpg`).
3. **Window security grilles** — ground/1st floors: welded scroll-and-circle pattern (`r4a0/103196.jpg`), plain vertical bars with mid-scrolls (`b5091.jpg`, `b185430.jpg`), white painted grid (`b89386.jpg`), diamond mesh on shops (`b66371.jpg`). Color black `#2A2A2A` or white.
4. **AC units + satellite dishes** — 1 AC per 2–3 windows on Soviet/modern stock, beige box `#DDD8CC` on L-brackets with drip streak; 0.9 m grey dishes bolted beside balconies (`b11614.jpg`, `b2246.jpg`, `b8444.jpg`). Plus catenary clotheslines on pulleys spanning balconies (`b34.jpg`, `b2246.jpg`).
5. **Shop layer** — fabric awning 0.8 m projection (white/striped, `b20206.jpg`), corrugated roller shutters `#C8C8C6` with graffiti, backlit fascia boxes (red `#C1272D`, `b81417.jpg`), product-poster panels on kiosks (thumb `r4a0/122735.jpg`).
6. **Cornice/parapet caps** — dentil stucco cornice + acanthus mascaron keystones on classic (`b20206.jpg`, `b47132.jpg`); corbelled brick dentil bands on brick stock (`b107619.jpg`); flat roof parapet with thin pipe guardrail on panels (thumb `r4a1/1251.jpg`).

Note for kit texturing: everything old is heavily weathered — rust bleeds under every steel fixing, black damp streaks under sills/slab edges, plaster spalling to brick (`b5091.jpg` is the decay-material reference: plaster `#C4BBA8` over brick `#9C5F45`).
